Privacy

We collect only what you type in the form, and use it only to pass your request to one local licensed septic company serving your area and to follow up on that request. We don't sell your information to anyone else, and we don't add you to any mailing list unless you tick the optional reminder box. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by email. This follows Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA) and anti‑spam law (CASL).
