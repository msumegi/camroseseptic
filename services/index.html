What a pump-out request can cover

Four jobs that come in from acreages, farms, cabins and shops. The company that does the work sets the price.

  • Acreage and farm septic tanks

    A regular house tank on a gravel road. Most of them are pumped every 2–4 years. A bigger tank, or fewer people in the house, stretches that out.

  • Holding tanks

    Cabins, shops and acreage homes with nowhere for the water to go. These fill on a schedule of weeks, not years, and the alarm means the tank is full.

  • Urgent backups and frozen lines

    Slow drains, a sounding alarm, or a line that freezes in January. Choose “As soon as possible” on the request and the company sees it first.

    If sewage is coming into the house, stop running water and don't flush.

  • Before selling a property

    A pump-out ahead of selling or buying a place. Buyers often ask for it before the deal closes. The company that comes sets the price.
