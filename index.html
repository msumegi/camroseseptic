How a request goes

  1. 1

    You give your name, a phone number, the nearest town or the legal land description, and when you need someone there.

  2. 2

    A local company calls about the day and the price. You decide whether to book.

  3. 3

    If sewage is already in the house, say so. The company sees that request first.

What usually needs pumping

  • A house tank that is due

    Most acreage tanks are pumped every 2 to 4 years, depending on size and how many people live there.

  • A holding tank that fills

    Cabins and shops often need a truck every few weeks, not every few years.

  • A line that froze

    January and February are when alarms and slow drains show up.

  • A sale on the way

    Buyers ask for a pump-out before the papers are signed.
