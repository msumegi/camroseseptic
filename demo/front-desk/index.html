Camrose Septic Rural Trades Front Desk

Rural Trades Front Desk

This is a phone and text package for a septic or trades shop. It answers the shop phone while the crew is on a job, texts people back when a call is missed, asks for a review after the work is done, and shows the month on one page.

You still make the booking. A person calls the customer back to confirm the day and the price. The package does not drive the truck, and it does not invent a time.

The sections below use made-up people, so you can see it without a live call. The example shop is Camrose Septic.

Example data

AI phone answering

A virtual assistant answers, says it is on a recorded line, and writes down the job. A person from the shop calls back to confirm the time and the price.

What the caller hears

Thanks for calling Camrose Septic, I'm the virtual assistant on a recorded line. I can take a pump‑out request. A person from the shop will call you back to confirm a time and a price. How can I help?

What the assistant writes down

  • Name
  • Location
  • Rural address or legal land description
  • How urgent it is
  • Callback number

A person calls back to confirm the day and the price. The assistant does not set either one.

Review request flow

After the job is finished, the customer gets one text asking for a review. Nothing goes out while the work is still open.

How it runs

  • 1

    Mark the job finished. In this example, Morgan Leung's tank pump‑out near Round Hill is done.

  • 2

    The next day, one text goes out. No text is sent before the work is finished.

  • 3

    The customer can tap the review link. On this demo the link goes nowhere.

  • 4

    A new review is counted on the monthly results page.
