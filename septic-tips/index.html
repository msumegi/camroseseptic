Septic tips

Looking after a rural tank

Short notes for acreages and farms, then the questions people ask.

Practical notes

  • Keep the lid reachable. Snow, a parked truck or stored equipment slows a pump-out. A clear path to the lid helps.
  • Write down the legal land description. It is the surest way to point someone to a rural yard.
  • In a backup, stop adding water. If sewage is backing up into the house, avoid running water and flushing until the tank is pumped.
  • A holding-tank alarm means the tank is full. Ease off on laundry and long showers, and arrange a pump-out. Additives do not empty a tank.

Common questions

How often should a septic tank be pumped?

Most acreage and farm septic tanks around Camrose County need pumping every 2 to 4 years, depending on tank size and how many people live in the home. Holding tanks need pumping whenever they fill, often every few weeks for a cabin or shop.

What does septic pumping cost around Camrose?

The company that calls sets the price, which depends on tank size, distance and access.

Can you help in winter?

Yes. Frozen lines and backups are common in January and February. Choose "As soon as possible (urgent)" on the request form and it will be flagged.

Is this request free?

Yes. Sending a request costs nothing and you are under no obligation to book.

Which areas do you cover?

Camrose and all of Camrose County, including Bawlf, New Norway, Hay Lakes, Kingman, Round Hill, Bittern Lake, Ohaton, Daysland and Tofield. Stettler County is covered too. The service area lists the towns.

Request a septic pump-out

Central Alberta Septic Requests runs this site; it is not a septic contractor; requests go to one local licensed septic company, which does the work and sets the price.
