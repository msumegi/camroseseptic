Septic tips

Looking after a rural tank

A few things that help on an acreage, then the questions people ask.

Practical notes

  • Keep the lid reachable. Snow, a parked truck or stored equipment slows a pump-out. A clear path to the lid helps.
  • Write down the legal land description. It is the surest way to point someone to a yard on a gravel road.
  • A holding-tank alarm means the tank is full. Ease off on laundry and long showers, and arrange a pump-out. Additives do not empty a tank.
  • January and February are the hard months. Frozen lines and slow drains are common then. Say so when you send the request.

Common questions

How often should a septic tank be pumped?

Most acreage and farm septic tanks around Camrose County need pumping every 2 to 4 years, depending on tank size and how many people live in the home. Holding tanks need pumping whenever they fill, often every few weeks for a cabin or shop.

What does septic pumping cost around Camrose?

The company that calls sets the price, which depends on tank size, distance and access.

Can you help in winter?

Yes. Frozen lines and backups are common in January and February. Choose "As soon as possible (urgent)" on the request form and the company sees it first.

Is this request free?

Yes. Sending a request costs nothing and you are under no obligation to book.

Which areas do you cover?

Camrose and Camrose County: Bawlf, New Norway, Hay Lakes, Kingman, Round Hill, Bittern Lake and Ohaton. Also Daysland in Flagstaff County, Tofield in Beaver County, and Stettler County (Stettler, Big Valley, Donalda, Erskine and Botha). The service area shows them by county.
